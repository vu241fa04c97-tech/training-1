Foundational Movements

Exercise Library

Master essential bodyweight and compound exercises with proper technique, targeted muscle activation, and proven set and repetition schemes.

Push Ups

Beginner
Difficulty Beginner
Sets & Repetitions 3 Sets × 12–15 Reps
Primary Target Chest, Shoulders & Triceps
Rest Interval 60 Seconds
Form Tip: Keep your body in a rigid plank line. Tuck elbows at a 45-degree angle rather than flaring wide.

Squats

Beginner
Difficulty Beginner
Sets & Repetitions 4 Sets × 12–15 Reps
Primary Target Quadriceps, Glutes & Hamstrings
Rest Interval 75 Seconds
Form Tip: Push your hips backward as if sitting down into a chair. Keep heels firmly planted and chest elevated.

Plank

Beginner
Difficulty Beginner
Sets & Repetitions 3 Sets × 45–60 Seconds
Primary Target Core, Transverse Abdominis & Spine
Rest Interval 45 Seconds
Form Tip: Place elbows directly underneath shoulders. Squeeze your glutes tightly and do not let your lower back sag.

Lunges

Intermediate
Difficulty Intermediate
Sets & Repetitions 3 Sets × 10 Reps / Leg
Primary Target Quads, Hamstrings & Balance
Rest Interval 60 Seconds
Form Tip: Step forward and lower until both knees form clean 90-degree angles. Keep your torso tall and vertical.

Crunches

Beginner
Difficulty Beginner
Sets & Repetitions 3 Sets × 20 Reps
Primary Target Upper Rectus Abdominis
Rest Interval 45 Seconds
Form Tip: Focus on lifting your shoulder blades off the floor via abdominal contraction rather than pulling your neck.

Burpees

Advanced
Difficulty Advanced
Sets & Repetitions 4 Sets × 10–12 Reps
Primary Target Full Body & Cardio Endurance
Rest Interval 90 Seconds
Form Tip: Move smoothly from a squat to a plank, perform a clean push-up, hop back into the squat, and explode upward.

Training Schedule

Weekly Workout Plan

Stay consistent with this proven 7-day split. Balance compound strength, core stability, cardiovascular stamina, and vital muscle recovery.

Monday Strength

Chest

Target the pectoral muscles and triceps with focused pressing movements and controlled flies.

Focus Area Upper & Lower Chest
Key Movements Bench Press, Push-Ups, Cable Flyes
Target Duration 45 Minutes
Tuesday Power

Legs

Build lower body power and athletic drive focusing on quads, hamstrings, and calves.

Focus Area Quads, Glutes & Hamstrings
Key Movements Squats, Lunges, Romanian Deadlifts
Target Duration 50 Minutes
Wednesday Core Stability

Abs

Strengthen the abdominal wall and obliques to reinforce posture, balance, and spine safety.

Focus Area Rectus Abdominis & Obliques
Key Movements Planks, Crunches, Leg Raises
Target Duration 35 Minutes
Thursday Active Recovery

Rest

Allow muscle tissues to repair, replenish glycogen stores, and recharge mental focus.

Focus Area Mobility & Full Recovery
Recommended Light Walking, Stretching, Hydration
Target Duration Full Day Off
Friday Hypertrophy

Arms

Isolate the biceps, triceps, and forearms with controlled contractions and supersets.

Focus Area Biceps, Triceps & Forearms
Key Movements Bicep Curls, Tricep Dips, Hammer Curls
Target Duration 45 Minutes
Saturday Endurance

Cardio

Elevate your heart rate, burn stored fat, and boost cardiovascular efficiency and lung capacity.

Focus Area Aerobic & Anaerobic Engine
Key Movements Interval Sprints, Jump Rope, Burpees
Target Duration 40 Minutes
Sunday Conditioning

Full Body

Integrate all major kinetic chains with a total-body circuit designed for functional stamina.

Focus Area Total Muscular Engagement
Key Movements Kettlebell Swings, Bodyweight Circuits
Target Duration 45 Minutes

Coaching Tip: Rest days are not skipped workouts—they are when muscle repair and protein synthesis actually happen. Stay hydrated and prioritize 7 to 8 hours of sleep each night.
